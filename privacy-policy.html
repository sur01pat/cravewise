CraveWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

CraveWise ("CraveWise", "we", "us", or "our") is a food and nutrition guidance application designed to help users make more informed choices about meals they want to eat, are eating, or can prepare from available ingredients.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CraveWise may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, CraveWise may process information you enter into the app, including:

  • Name, if you choose to provide it.
  • Dietary preference, such as omnivore, vegetarian, or vegan.
  • Allergies and foods you choose to avoid.
  • Cooking confidence and budget preference.
  • Foods, meals, ingredients, cravings, meal preferences, and restaurant choices you enter.
  • Pantry ingredients and meal information used to generate meal ideas.

2. How We Use Information

We use information you provide to:

  • Provide personalized meal and food suggestions.
  • Adapt recommendations to dietary preferences, allergies, ingredients, time, effort, and other choices you provide.
  • Provide nutrition-related educational content and practical meal guidance.
  • Save features you explicitly choose to save, where supported.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

3. AI-Powered Recommendations

CraveWise uses Google's Gemini generative AI services for certain personalized recommendations and food-related analysis. Information that you submit to an AI-powered feature may be transmitted to the applicable Google AI service to generate a response.

We do not use CraveWise to make medical diagnoses or provide emergency medical advice. AI-generated information is intended for general food and nutrition guidance and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or transmit information only as reasonably necessary to provide the application's features, including with service providers that process information on our behalf and AI/cloud service providers used by CraveWise.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of CraveWise, our users, or others.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through CraveWise. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested features, maintain the application, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may choose not to provide optional information. If you want to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of CraveWise, contact us using the address below. We will process deletion requests subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

CraveWise is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Third-Party Services

CraveWise may use third-party infrastructure and services to operate the application, process AI requests, provide hosting, analytics, security, or other technical functions. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page. Continued use of CraveWise after an update means that the updated policy applies to your use of the application.
